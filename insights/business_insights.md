# Business Insights

## Portfolio Snapshot

- 38,576 loan applications are represented in the dataset.
- Approximately $435.8M was funded.
- Approximately $473.1M was received.
- 86.2% of applications fall into the project's Good Loan definition (Fully Paid or Current).
- 13.8% of applications are Charged Off.

## Portfolio Quality

Charged-off loans represent 5,333 applications and approximately $65.5M in funded amount.

## Loan Mix

36-month loans represent the majority of applications, at approximately 73.2%, while 60-month loans represent approximately 26.8%.

Debt consolidation is the dominant loan purpose. Rent and Mortgage are the largest home-ownership groups.

## Dashboard Takeaway

The report provides descriptive portfolio analysis across loan status, time, geography, loan characteristics and borrower characteristics. These views can support further investigation of portfolio risk, product mix and concentration.
